# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Neighbourhood and postcode directory:** `createNeighbourhoods(source)` and `GeoEnv.neighbourhoods` (optional). A postcode fills a missing province, and the district when it is unambiguous; `search.validate` returns a `postcode_mismatch` note (new `FixField` `'postcode'`) when the postcode does not fit the typed neighbourhood; neighbourhood suggestions also come from the directory; results gain `components.postcode` when the map data has none. Without the option nothing changes. The pack is built from a CSV with `npm run data:neighbourhoods`; the data is not shipped (`docs/neighbourhoods.md`).

## 0.8.0 (2026-11-10)

- **Own visual design:** a new palette (cool grey-blue ground, sage parks, slate water, amber motorways; its own dark side), group colours that are no longer system colours, rounded-square category badges with the name below, new location mark (`locate-*` images), rounded-square `LocateButton` and `CompassButton` in the brand colour, new `PlaceCard`, `ScaleBar` and `HereStrip`, flat extrusion shading for 3D buildings and a flat default camera (`DEFAULT_PITCH` is 0). Comparison gates no longer measure against another map; `KF6.2` checks label contrast. Layer ids and the public API are unchanged.
- **Traffic on the map and on routes:** slow roads are drawn as short coloured stretches on only some roads of a kind (orange for slow, red for heavy), side streets stay faint, and flowing big roads get a light green dash; the dark map has a bluer ground with stronger main roads. `geo.route.route({ at })` returns `traffic.spans` (which share of the route is smooth, busy, heavy or jam), `useNavigation` carries them and `TrafficRouteLayer` draws the route black (light grey on the dark map) with the slow parts coloured.
- **Observed speeds for Istanbul routes:** with `at` set, route delays and `traffic.spans` use the hourly average speeds the Istanbul open data portal publishes per area (about 1 km cells, weekday, Saturday and Sunday), weighted by road class; elsewhere the typical-time model stays. `scripts/build-traffic-profile.ts` rebuilds `src/core/traffic/data/ibb-istanbul.json` from the monthly CSV files. These are past averages, not live traffic.
- **Observed speeds on the Istanbul map:** `scripts/split-road-cells.ts <packs> <out>` cuts the road lines of the Istanbul tiles at the borders of the open-data cells and tags each piece with its `cell`; the map then colours every piece by its own cell's observed slowdown (one layer set per slowdown step), and roads of tiles without cells keep the typical-time model.
- **Traffic measurement:** `createRouter({ traffic })` takes the speed source of a region (default: the built-in Istanbul profile); `npx tsx scripts/measure-traffic.ts --check holdout|bands|spatial|eta` scores the rule, a city curve and the cell profile against a month the profile has not seen.
- **Route delays outside Istanbul fitted to measured speeds:** the hourly curve and the class slowdowns behind `at` come from the Istanbul open data (vehicle-weighted, measured against the night speed of each cell, peak slowdown 0.33 on a motorway). Rush hour delays drop from roughly +250 % to +50 % on a motorway in a big city; `traffic.index` follows the measured curve (weekday midday about 0.62, night about 0.03). The traffic layer of the map keeps its own curve.
- **Door-level address search, step by step** (`adres-v2`): new building layer (`buildings2` part), building-aware door prediction, unit and block resolution, place and landmark search, validation, reverse geocoding and typeahead. Details per area below.
  - `geo.search.resolve`: a missing house number is placed on the building the numbering leads to (`radiusM`, `notes`); a written block, floor and flat become `place.unit` (`resolveUnit`, `formatUnit` in six languages, notes `block_not_found`, `floor_out_of_range`, `door_out_of_range`, `floor_door_mismatch`); a place name with a district or a kind of place with an area ("eczane Fethiye") finds places, ranked above the district itself and by distance when `near` is given; "X karşısı / yanı / arkası / köşesi" (and `opposite`, `next to`, `behind`, `corner`) give the building across, beside, behind or at the corner of a landmark (`place.relation`).
  - `geo.search.validate(text)`: `ok`, `incomplete`, `conflict` or `not_found` with notes (`missing_neighbourhood`, `missing_number`, `district_mismatch`, `street_not_in_neighbourhood`, `number_out_of_range`, `ambiguous_street`, `parity_side_unknown`, `outside_installed_regions`, the unit and landmark notes), a correction for each, the standard address (`standardAddress`, UAVT order, six languages) and "did you mean" for streets, neighbourhoods and districts. Note, kind and status texts in six languages (`noteText`, `kindText`, `statusText`).
  - `geo.search.reverse(point, { heading, accuracyM })`: the building the point is in (or the nearest within 25 m, up to the GPS accuracy; the one ahead of `heading` first), its known door, else an estimated number with a range ("No ~12"), side, the buildings around (±2) with their numbers, floors, entrances and site (`place.building`, `place.around`, `place.side`, `place.numberRange`).
  - `geo.search.autocomplete(text, options)`: the street fragment after a neighbourhood is read, the streets of a typed neighbourhood are listed, a typed number is completed with the known doors, doors carry `unitHint` (blocks, floors, flats) and every suggestion a `completion` text; `session` (`createTypeSession`) keeps the previous key's work, `recent`/`saved` places, `near`, `deep: false` and `budgetMs` for the heavy second stage.
- **Packs and manifests:** `docs/address-format.md`. New part kind `buildings2` (format 2; building footprint area, levels, street chain, side, sequence, entrances, settlements). The release now has two manifests: `manifest-v1.json` (old kinds only, what 0.3-0.7 SDKs read) and `manifest-v2.json` (everything, `minSdk` 0.8.0). This SDK asks for `manifest-v2.json` first and falls back to `manifest-v1.json` on 404; it skips file kinds it does not know instead of refusing the manifest. The default parts of a new install include `buildings2`; an installed province gets it with `geo.regions.download(id, { parts })`. `store.openPart('buildings2')` opens it.
- **Central learning** (`learned` part, format 1, manifest-v2 only): delivered doors, the floor layout of a building (doors per floor, basement, observed floor ranges), entrances and street-name hints, built by `scripts/learned-build.ts` from anonymised exports (`pipeline/real/records.ts`). `createGeo` adds the part as a point layer after the device's own learning: a trusted learned point answers a door the map does not have (or beats the map door with three independent sources), `place.unit.floor` is estimated from the learned layout (`source: 'learned'`), and a street that is not on the map but was delivered to by three couriers resolves to the delivered place (`precision: 'street'`, `radiusM`). `learned` is part of the default parts. Schema and rules: `docs/address-format.md`.
- **Street hints for streets the map lacks** (`learned` part, tables `hint` and `way`): a street that is in no address pack but on the business addresses of the directory (or on municipal and open-data lists) gets an approximate hint, so "Fatiha Ykş Sokak No:13 Bitlis Merkez" resolves to the street (`precision: 'street'`, `approximate: true`, `source: 'learned'`, `radiusM`) instead of the district centre. With a street line and at least four known door numbers (`way`) a numbered query is placed along the line (`precision: 'interpolated'`). The district is matched by the pack's name, so centre districts ("Bitlis Merkez") find their hints; a street typed with a province but no district lists the districts that have it (up to five, the best sourced first). A typed neighbourhood far from the hint rules it out. Older SDKs ignore the `way` table.
- **Second business source for doors and streets:** the directory part and the building numbering also read `fsq/<plate>.rows.jsonl` in the work folder (Foursquare OS Places, Apache-2.0). These rows only add doors and street lines; their businesses are not listed and a door or street the first source already has keeps its point. `scripts/fsq-eval.ts split|build|measure`, `scripts/fsq-buildings-eval.ts`. Directory doors 787,061 → 1,228,746, median distance to OSM doors 15 → 16 m.
- **Faster address search on the phone:** street hints are read once per district, an empty learned door table is no longer queried, the doors and lines of the candidate streets are read in one batch and kept per street, buildings are kept between calls, and the street names of a region are held in memory after `geo.search.warm` (up to 150,000 names). Results are unchanged; a search makes about 20 database calls instead of about 90 (Istanbul, iPhone 11: median 143 → about 50 ms).
- **Delivery stop point:** `deliveryStop(trace, intended, confirm)` (`@yuksi/geo` core) finds the place a courier stood still (speed < 1 m/s clusters of the last 90 s of GPS) nearest to the customer's pin and the map point, so the delivery point no longer has to be the GPS fix of the moment the button was pressed. The central learning build uses it (stop over pin over confirm GPS), turns it into an entrance candidate next to a building, and never stores the trace; the courier's chosen floor feeds the building layout.
- **Route arrival at the door:** `geo.route.route(from, to, { toPlace })` and `geo.route.directions(..., { toPlace })` end on the road of the place's own street (the street's lines from the address pack, a road edge within 10 m of them and within 150 m of the target) instead of the nearest road, aim at `place.unit.entrance` or `place.entrance` when there is one, and return `arrival` (`side` left or right of the heading, `offM`, `target` `'entrance' | 'door'`, `onChain`). The last step of `directions` says "Hedef sağda/solda" (`turn.arrive-left`, `turn.arrive-right`, six languages) with the block and entrance (`formatEntrance`). `geo.plan.plan` takes `{ id, place }` stops (`PlaceStop`), orders them with each stop snapped to its own street and puts `arrival` on each leg. A place without an entrance gets one from the learned part (the entrance couriers agree on) or from the building pack (the one holding the written flat, the main one, else the one nearest the street) and the last step names its entrance; the spoken cue "X sonra varış" says the side too. Without `toPlace` nothing changes. `snapPoint` takes an optional `prefer` (`Prefer`) and the snap says `onChain`.
- **Written units:** `parseAddress` reads the entrance (`Giriş 2`, `2. giriş`, `Entrance 2`, `Eingang 3`, `parsed.entrance`) and `resolveUnit` takes that entrance of the building (`UnitInput.entrance`; `pickEntrance(list, door, ref)`); `giriş kat` is the ground floor; `kat -2` and `2. bodrum kat` are the second basement floor (`2. Bodrum Kat`, `Basement 2`, ... `formatUnit`) instead of the first; `çatı katı` is the top floor of the building with a range (`isRoofFloor`).
- **Places with a street:** "Starbucks Bağdat Caddesi Kadıköy" finds the Starbucks whose address is on that street (before: the district); it runs only when the street part did not resolve to a door and there is no house number. Category words (`eczane`, `hastane`, ...) are also known in German, Russian, Greek and Arabic.
- **Smaller fixes:** `validate` says `number_not_on_street` ("this number was not found on this street", a street result for a typed number) and keeps `number_out_of_range` for a number past the last building of the street; `geo.search.warm(plate)` opens a region (and its polygons and building index) before the first query so the app can pay the opening cost in the background (about 17 ms on a laptop, several times that on a phone); `deliveryStop` takes `onSide` (the street line and the side of the door) and prefers a stop on the door's side of the street, so a car stopped across the street does not win; `scripts/learned-build.ts` reports neighbourhoods that couriers write for places lying in another neighbourhood (`hoodSuggestions`, at least three records).
- **Building names:** a building with a name ("Nurol Plaza", "Dadaş Plaza", a mosque) is found by `resolve` (district from the typed text, ranked below a place of the same name that the directory knows) and offered by `autocomplete` from three letters on. The `buildings2` part has a new optional table `building_name` (`docs/address-format.md`); the format version stays 2 and older SDKs ignore it, a pack without it simply has no name search. `openBuildings2(...).named(prefix, limit)` reads it.
- **Address server and client:** `server/` (`npm run serve:geo`, `docs/server.md`) serves the packs of a release over HTTP with the same search core: `POST /v1/geocode`, `/v1/validate`, `/v1/autocomplete`, `GET /v1/reverse`, `/v1/places`, `/health`, `/version`; API keys, a token bucket per key, a lazily opened least-recently-used set of provinces (a closed region is closed after a grace period so a query in flight is not cut off), and a log that never holds a query text. `createGeoClient({ baseUrl, key })` (`@yuksi/geo/core`) is the matching client with the types of `geo.search`; `createGeo({ online: { baseUrl, key } })` asks the server when a province is not installed (`region_missing`, no suggestions, no reverse result) and keeps the offline answer when the server cannot be reached. `npm run build:server` bundles the server into `dist/geo-server.mjs`; a `Dockerfile` is included.
- **Street and area shapes on the map:** `geo.search.resolve(text, { geometry: true })` adds `place.geometry` and `place.bbox` to street, neighbourhood and district results (a province gets a `bbox`), and `geo.search.geometry(place)` gives the same for any such place (for example the row of a suggestion). A street is every part of that street in its district joined into one multi-line (independent of the neighbourhood; `local` lists the lines inside the typed neighbourhood), thinned to about 2 m (Douglas-Peucker, at most 1500 points) with the range of known door numbers (`numbers`); an area is its boundary polygons thinned to about 10 m. `fitBounds(bbox, viewport)` gives the camera for a box (the whole of a long street, at most zoom 17 for a short one), `<PlaceGeometryLayer place theme />` draws the line, the boundary and a "Bağdat Caddesi · No 1-538" label, `usePlaceGeometry(place)` loads the shape of a place. The address server has `POST /v1/geometry` and a `geometry` option on `geocode`; `createGeoClient` has `geometry(place)`.
- **Build:** new steps `bina izleri` (Overture building footprints and OSM building tags per province), `bina paketleri 2`, `adsız yollar` and `sokak adları` (street names derived from business addresses for unnamed OSM roads); the business step now runs before the address packs because the derived names go into them. `npx tsx scripts/measure-packs.ts --check determinism-adres` repeats these steps and compares the packs and intermediate files byte by byte. The validator checks `buildings2` (format, counts, region box, levels, street chains against the address pack, entrances) and that manifest-v1 carries no new kind and manifest-v2 is a superset of it.
- Smaller changes: `validate`, `reverse` and `autocomplete` options are optional and old calls behave as before except that results for a place name now come before the area-only district result, a street typo is no longer searched in the whole street table for a name with no number or kind (long place names are not mistaken for streets), and a street's neighbours are read once per district.

## 0.7.2 (2026-11-08)

- Patch updates: when a province is updated, a file that changed is downloaded as a patch against the installed file instead of in full. The manifest lists the patches of a file in the optional `patches` field (`from` is the sha256 of the old file); the build keeps the last 3 releases (`GEO_PATCHES`, `0` turns it off) and writes only patches that apply back to the exact new file and are smaller than 85% of the full download. On the real releases an update from the previous week downloads 1900 → 220 MB over 81 provinces. Older SDKs ignore the field.
- The patch file (`.zpatch`, `docs/patch-format.md`) holds 8 MiB slices, each compressed against a range of the old file. The phone applies it in JavaScript (`applyPatchFile`) with one reused work buffer and a pause every few milliseconds, then checks the sha256 of the result. The result and a patch over 8 MiB are checked by their block hashes (`blocks`, native `expo-crypto`) like a full download, not by a whole-file hash in JavaScript, which blocked the JS thread for 1.3 s per 2 MB on an iPhone 11. If a patch cannot be fetched or applied, only that file is downloaded in full; a lost connection or a cancel is not retried as a full download. The old file stays readable until the update is committed.
- `RegionStatus.updateBytes` is the real download size of an update and `RegionStatus.lastUpdate` (`UpdateReport`: `patched`, `fellBack`, `bytes`) tells how the last update went. `PackConfig.patches: false` turns patching off. The update line of the region list shows `updateBytes`.
- Downloading or deleting many small files (the font files of the base pack, 777 requests) hands the thread back to the engine every 12 ms. On an iPhone 11 a base pack update used to hold the JS thread for 4.8 s and 2.3 s in a row (about 9 ms of file system calls per file, no gap in between); now the longest gap is 86 ms and the update takes as long as before.
- The zstd decoder can start with a prefix (`decompressInPlaceSteps`, `decompressPrefix`); long copies are faster and `rzfh` no longer compares a typed array with `==`.

## 0.7.0 (2026-11-07)

- Routes across provinces: when the start and the end lie in different provinces, `geo.route.route` joins the route packs of the installed provinces between them (`pickChain`, `borderSamples`, `crossingsOf`, `solveChain` in `src/core/route/chain.ts`). Crossings are found in the 10 km band both packs cover and the quickest combination is chosen; the result lists the provinces in `regions`. A province on the way that is not installed still ends in `outside_region`.
- Typical traffic (`src/core/traffic/model.ts`): a built-in weekday, Saturday and Sunday profile of the congestion in Turkish time, scaled by road class and by how busy the province is. It works offline and knows nothing about live events.
  - `geo.route.route(from, to, { at })` returns `durationS` with the delay of that hour and `traffic: { index, freeFlowS, delayS, detour }`. In the rush hour it also tries eight detours through calmer roads and takes one that is at least 5% quicker. Routes across provinces get the delay of each province. Without `at` nothing changes.
  - `useMapStyle({ traffic: true })` draws the slow roads yellow, orange and red (`traffic@` layers under the labels, `TRAFFIC_COLOURS`), refreshed every 15 minutes. `useNavigation` asks for traffic-aware routes.
  - `congestion`, `bandOf`, `slowdownOf`, `mapSlowdown`, `regionBusyness`, `trafficBucket`, `localTime`.
- Route packs are read in slices of 8 ms (`readRoutePackAsync`) and the steps of a route across provinces pause in between, so touches are handled while a pack opens. Hermes: longest step 252 ms (Denizli pack) → 12 ms.
- The small files of the base pack (fonts) download eight at a time instead of one by one: 777 requests no longer take minutes.
- `useMapStyle` builds the style again only when the installed provinces or the base pack change, not on every download progress tick.
- Provinces that are not installed: `MissingRegionsLayer` draws their border with a hatched fill and fades it out when the download ends; `regionOutlines`, `Borders.provincePolygons`, `isMissing`, `missingAt`, `missingPlates`, `regionBytes`.
- Driving: `trackRoute`, `dueCue`, `cueText`, `headingTo` and `useNavigation` (progress along the route, next turn, spoken cues, off route detection and a bird's eye fallback).

## 0.6.0 (2026-11-04)

- The Expo config plugin is `app.plugin.cjs`. As `app.plugin.js` in a package of ES modules Expo could not load it, so `"plugins": ["@yuksi/geo"]` failed with `INVALID_PLUGIN_TYPE`.
- A half downloaded zstd file is kept when the app restarts, so the download goes on from where it stopped instead of starting again from zero. Removing a province also deletes its zstd leftovers.
- Optional MapLibre Tile packs: `GEO_TILE_FORMAT=mlt` converts the map and business tiles after pruning (`pipeline/build/tile-encode.ts`; feature ids are dropped and FastPFOR and FSST are off, because the MapLibre Native decoders cannot read those encodings). The style reads `*.mlt.pmtiles` as MLT and the manifest then asks for SDK 0.6.0. 81 provinces 1366.8 → 1227.9 MB on top of pruning. `npm run mlt:check` decodes every tile with the MapLibre Native decoder. Off by default.
- Route pack version 2 stores an arc and its exact reverse once (`docs/route-format.md`): 81 provinces 354.4 → 299.5 MB on the phone, the same routes. The SDK reads versions 1 and 2; the build writes version 2 (`GEO_ROUTE_FORMAT=1` keeps the old one) and sets `minSdk` to 0.6.0.
- Opening a zstd pack is about three times faster on the phone (Hermes: 4.1-6.2 → 13.5-16.4 MB/s) and never holds the JS thread for long (longest step 541 → 70 ms). The fzstd decoder is now part of the SDK (`src/core/packs/fzstd.ts`) and keeps its window in a double buffer instead of moving 8 MB for every block; the `fzstd` dependency is gone.
- Smaller map packs: the build drops tile features no style variant draws and properties no layer reads (`pipeline/build/tile-prune.ts`), and empties glyph ranges no label uses. Istanbul tiles 68.9 → 58.9 MB, Bursa 36.1 → 28.6 MB, business tiles about 43% smaller; headless renders of 980 views match the unpruned packs. `GEO_PRUNE=0` turns it off.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-03)

Apps linked with `file:` build it once. `react`, `react-native`, `expo` and MapLibre stay peer dependencies.
- `docs/licences.md`: what the package and the packs are made of and what each licence asks.
- `npm run sdk-check` now installs the packed package into a blank app (`--expo 58.0.22` moves the app to another Expo version first).
- `geo.search.nearby({ point, group, limit, radiusM })`: places of one group (health, pharmacy, safety, money, fuel, worship, lodging, market, public) around a point, nearest first, from the address pack; `useNearby`.
- `geo.route.directions(from, to, { language })`: the route with step by step text ("In 200 m, turn right onto Moda Caddesi"), roundabout exit numbers, merged bends and street names from the address pack, in six languages (`buildManeuvers`, `directionsOf`). Silent: no voice.
- `PlaceCard` and `usePlaceAt`: the badge nearest to a tap within 24 px (the more important group wins a tie), its name, group and address, with "Directions here" and "Copy address".
- `CompassButton` (only while the map is turned), `ScaleBar` (6 languages, rounded lengths), `HereStrip` and `useHereLabel` ("Near Caferağa Mah., Moda Caddesi 12").
- Theme `auto` for `useMapStyle`: follows the system appearance and builds the style once.
- `RegionDownloadSheet`: last update date and "waiting for mobile data approval" (`consentFor`).
- Device tests `sdk.place-card`, `sdk.compass`, `sdk.nearby`, `sdk.maneuvers`, `sdk.scale-bar`.

- Place names: the Turkish name of Overture comes first, then a same-group OSM name within 50 m (`place_names.py`, `osm_names.py`); an English pattern ("Railway X", "X Station") is replaced when the OSM place is found; a name that is only a category word ("Eğitim", "Park", "Eczane") is blank and the place keeps only its badge.
- A place whose name ends in cami, camii, mescit, eczane or hastane gets that glyph when its category says nothing definite (`glyphFromName`).
- `scripts/glyph-audit.ts`: random sample per province, name, category and glyph table, with suspects listed first.
- `scripts/verify-packs.ts`: checks the manifest signature and the size and sha256 of every file.
- Chain position audit also counts an OSM address point within 30 m as independent evidence (`adres`).

- 3D buildings: a `fill-extrusion` layer from zoom 16 (height from `height`, else 6 m; rises in over 0.6 zoom, opacity 0.85); `map.buildings3d: false` turns it off.
- Dark base map (`DARK_BASE`): ground, water, parks, buildings, roads and label colours; names and group inks keep 4.5:1 against the ground.
- Cemeteries and sports grounds have their own tones (`map.base.cemetery`, `map.base.sports`), no new layers.
- Road shields from the `ref` property (the tiles carry no `shield_text`): `D 100`, `O-1`, `E 80` and `34-12` become state, motorway, European and provincial shields (`shieldOf`, `geo-shield-*` images, `npm run icons:shields`).
- Transit: `pipeline/extract/extract_transit.py` reads live subway, light rail, tram, train and ferry routes and named stations from OSM; the style draws them (`regions[].transitPath`) from zoom 11 (lines) and 13 (stations). The transit pack part is not built or shipped yet.
- Device tests `sdk.map-3d`, `sdk.map-dark`, `sdk.map-transit`.

- Street names: letter spacing 0.1 em, wider symbol spacing and padding, small streets start at zoom 14.5.
- Neighbourhood names are written in Turkish capitals by `upperTr` (`i` becomes `İ`, `ı` becomes `I`) with grey 0.12 em spaced letters; MapLibre's own `text-transform` does not know the Turkish rule.
- Long place names: wrapped at 7 em; a name that would pass two lines gets a short form (`abbreviateName`, `ns` in the business tiles) that Turkish maps prefer.
- Font option `font: 'inter' | 'noto'` for `useMapStyle`, `map.style` and `GeoHoodLabels`. Inter glyph files are built with `npm run fonts` (merged with the Noto glyphs, because a glyph file cannot join two fonts); the base pack build copies them in only with `GEO_FONT=inter` (they add about 19 MB, and the base pack limit is 30 MB).
- Device tests `sdk.map-languages`, `sdk.map-label-lines`, `sdk.shot-tour`.

- `useMapStyle({ view, maxRegions })`: only the provinces near the view are in the style (`regionsForView`), so the style no longer grows with every installed province.
- Address packs: at most four open provinces (least recently used first) and idle provinces are let go; `hoods()` reads neighbourhood names without keeping databases open (`PackStore.release`, `PackStore.peek`).
- Business tiles carry the badge glyph, tier and group (`k`, `t`, `grp`); the style uses them when present (`placeProps`), `scripts/make-alt-packs.ts` builds a second pack root for comparisons.

- Mobile data consent: with `autoDownload: 'always'` a province over `cellularAskMb` (default 50 MB) is asked about before it downloads on mobile data (`decision.action === 'consent'`, `RegionConsentSheet`, `accept`, `waitForWifi`); `download(id, { wifiOnly })`, `downloadBytes`, `connection`, `onConnection`; `GeoNativeConfig.connectivity`.
- A download that was cut when the app went to the background is tried again when the app returns to the front and at every ten-minute location read, even when the position did not move.
- Location timestamps are read in seconds (iOS) and milliseconds (Android); an older position is used as a fallback up to half an hour when nothing fresher arrives.

- `GeoHoodLabels` draws neighbourhood names; the style no longer waits for them, so the map loads its style once.
- Camera helpers `safeView`, `normalBearing`, `clampPitch`, `jumpTilted`.
- `useLocateMe`: `openSettings()`, asks again after a refusal, stops when the permission is taken back, ignores cached positions older than two minutes.
- Cargo and post branches (Yurtiçi, Aras, MNG, PTT...) show from zoom 16 like pharmacies; chain places carry a confidence of 90, which the stricter tier hid at street level.
- Example app: scene life cycle for iOS 27; device tests can repeat (`--repeat`).
